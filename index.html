About SkillBridge

SkillBridge is a smart freelancing platform designed to help freelancers find suitable job opportunities based on their skills. It uses a rule-based matching system to calculate the percentage of skills matched between a freelancer and a job.

Technologies Used:

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, localStorage and sessionStorage
